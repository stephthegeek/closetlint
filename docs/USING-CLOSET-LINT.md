## Browse and collections

Filter your closet by category, tags, favorites, and other recorded details. Open a collection to browse its pieces. **All**, **Closet**, and the site title leave the collection filter.

Use the **…** menus on piece, outfit, and collection cards for more actions.

## Piece details

Open a piece to edit its name, photos, category, size, and other details. Categories and subcategories organize the kinds of pieces in your closet. Tags describe qualities such as color, fabric, style, occasion, and season, helping you filter your closet and find outfit combinations.

Add the details that are useful to you and leave unknown information blank. Layering choices describe how a piece can be worn with others; fabric weight and stretch describe how it feels and fits. **Metal & finish** adds metal tones and finishes to jewelry, watches, and bags, such as rose gold, copper, or antiqued metal.

## Outfit board

Arrange pieces on the board, then choose **Save outfit**. **Save changes** updates an outfit you are editing; **Save a copy** creates a separate outfit.

Autosave keeps your working board, but does not update a saved outfit. **Other drafts → Resume** returns to work kept when switching boards. Wait for successful autosave before closing; drafts are included in full wardrobe backups. If a save fails, keep the tab open and use Retry.

Opening a saved outfit shows its preview and leaves your current board in place. Choose **Edit outfit** to work on it. You can also log a wear or export its image from the preview.

## Wear logging and the calendar

Log a saved outfit with **Wore it**, or log individual pieces from their own pages. Scheduling an outfit is a plan, not a wear log.

Open a calendar day to see its outfits and other worn pieces, log an outfit, or manage that day’s records. **Save as outfit** creates an outfit from the individually logged pieces shown for that day. Removing a day from an outfit also removes the piece dates added by that outfit, while preserving independently recorded or shared dates. Individual piece dates can be managed separately.

Calendar thumbnails give a quick preview of each day. **+** shows how many additional pieces or outfits are logged; open the day to see everything.

Piece pages show the last 10 days of wear and a count for the last 30 days. Browse cards show an icon for hot pieces; **Hot** and **Very hot** filters help find them. 🔥 starts at three logged days in thirty days; ❤️‍🔥 starts at eight. Wears can be on any days; they do not need to be consecutive. Use **Today** to add or remove an individual wear. For a wear recorded through an outfit, open the day’s details to manage it.

On a future calendar day, create a dated outfit or schedule one you have saved. **Save & schedule** saves the new outfit and its date together. Returning to the calendar without saving does not schedule it.

## Batch workspaces

**Add → Bulk import pieces** and **Menu → Utilities → Tidy photos** open separate pages. Leaving a page keeps its unfinished review. Check the draft notice before closing or reloading: it confirms recovery is stored or offers Retry.

These browser drafts belong to this browser and installation. They do not transfer between devices and are not included in wardrobe backups. If draft storage fails, finish the work or use the offered download/recovery options before closing. **Start over** or **Choose pieces again** discards unfinished review after confirmation, without removing already saved pieces.

Direct imports keep their saved progress on the installation. See [AI assistant imports](AI-ASSISTANT.md#ai-assistant-imports) for continuation and duplicate handling.

## Bookmarks, tabs, and devices

Bookmark or copy the address shown by the app to return to a piece, outfit, collection, trip, or calendar month. The address belongs to that installation; sharing it does not share or copy the wardrobe.

Back and Forward follow your navigation. Browse filters and scroll position can return with browser history; a newly opened bookmark does not necessarily reproduce those temporary filters.

Links support the browser’s Open in new tab action. A new tab opens saved data, without copying unsaved edits. Save before switching devices. If an older tab refuses a save, preserve its unfinished work before refreshing.

On home Wi-Fi, use the address printed by the wardrobe computer. On a phone, `localhost` refers to the phone itself. See [installation](GETTING-STARTED.md#start-the-app) for phone access.


## Photos

An ordinary photo is enough to add a piece. Use your camera, a screenshot, or an available product photo. You can keep several views, including model, isolated, detail, and care label photos.

### Main photos and framing

Open a piece and expand **Photo options** to add or replace photos, crop them, adjust swatches, or choose the main photo. Viewing a gallery thumbnail does not change the main photo.

The main photo appears in your closet and on boards. **Thumbnail framing** lets you show the whole photo or fill its frame with a crop. Tap the large photo to enlarge it.

Each piece can have up to 12 photos, including cutouts and care labels. Saved images are limited to 1600 pixels on their longest edge. Keep camera originals separately if you need full resolution. Full wardrobe backups include all saved photo versions.

### Optional background removal

Background removal creates a transparent cutout and keeps the original. It does not redraw a garment or remove a person wearing it. Choose **Remove background**, review the result, then keep the cutout or discard it. Lace, fine jewelry, and sheer fabric can be difficult; keeping the original is fine.

You can also start background removal from a selected board piece. Accepting a cutout makes it the piece’s main photo throughout Closet Lint; switch back from the piece page if needed.

For several pieces, open **Menu → Utilities → Tidy photos**. Review the current photo and cutout for each piece, choose what to keep, and save. Pieces left on Current photo are not suggested again in Tidy photos; you can still retry from their piece pages. Pieces whose main photo is already a cutout are skipped.

Wait for the draft notice before closing an unfinished review. Draft recovery belongs to this browser and is separate from wardrobe backups. See [batch workspaces](USING-CLOSET-LINT.md#batch-workspaces).

### Care label references

Open **Care → Add care label photo**, or mark an existing view **Care label** in Photo options. Washing, Drying, and Care note are optional. Photo uploads save immediately; other form edits save with **Save changes**.

Care labels can be enlarged but are excluded from main garment photos, boards, swatches, Tidy photos, and background removal. They count toward the 12-photo limit and are included in backups. Removing a photo offers Undo.

For optional tool installation, see [Getting started](GETTING-STARTED.md#start-the-app). For individual product photo requests, see [AI assistant photo preparation](AI-ASSISTANT.md#prepare-individual-photos-for-existing-pieces).

## Trips and packing

Open **Menu → Trips & events**. Add a trip for travel and a packing list, or add an event to schedule a saved outfit for one day.

### Plan a trip

1. Enter a name, destination, and dates. A name is enough to save unfinished setup and return later.
2. Choose the day types, extra evening outfits, bag, and laundry availability. Adjust individual days when plans differ.
3. Choose which pieces to consider. You can include saved outfits, stored pieces, and wishlist pieces, and add pieces or outfits you are bringing for sure.
4. Build the suggestions. Review them, swap pieces, and pin the outfits you intend to bring.

Only pinned outfits go into a new trip’s packing list. Explicitly added pieces and selected basics are included too. Suggestions are not marked packed automatically. Save failures keep your choices available for Retry.

### Weather and suitability

Typical weather uses recent seasonal averages, not a forecast. Check the destination and temperatures, or enter them yourself. Consider your activities and the range between daytime and evening conditions.

Matching uses the details in your closet. A shoe category does not establish walking comfort or waterproofing, and a style tag does not guarantee a dress code is met. Review suggested clothing and equipment for your actual plans.

### Basics and activities

The planner suggests underwear, bras, socks, and sleepwear based on the pieces recorded in your closet. Review these basics and adjust them to your routine.

Quantities are starting points. Use **Choose** to change the pieces or quantity; zero omits that group. Missing matches refer to the catalog and selected filters, rather than everything you own.

Add swimming or exercise to include reminders for those activities. Check that you have the pieces you need for each activity, including anything you have not added to your closet.

Laundry availability reduces suggested quantities. Adjust them to when you can wash and dry your clothes. Extra evening outfits use dressy suggestions.

### Pack and export

Check items off as you pack. **Wear on the way** separates suggested travel clothing from bag contents; change the bulky item travel option if it does not suit you. Stored and wishlist pieces have separate pull/buy reminders.

The bag bar estimates space for clothing. Allow room for toiletries and other belongings, and check luggage dimensions and weight separately.

Use **Export packing list** to print, save a PDF through your browser, or copy/download Markdown text. Print/PDF can include small photos; outfit combinations are optional in both formats. The export reflects the current packing selection.

Changed trip choices may need **Rebuild suggestions** before the list reflects them. Rebuilding keeps pinned outfits; check quantities and other explicit choices afterward.

## Wishlist

Your choices set the ranking order. Use Ranking to arrange the list or Head-to-head to compare two pieces. Compatibility callouts use recorded colors, tags, favorites, Lab ratings, and wears. Missing details limit that evidence; a possible match or overlap is not a purchase recommendation. Saved notes may be older AI assistant advice.

## Themes and settings

Open **Menu → View settings**.

- **Soft Hardware:** pixel accents and monospace controls.
- **Field Notes:** warm paper colors and serif headings.
- **Night Bloom:** plum colors and arched photo frames.

Each theme offers light, dark, and system appearance. Choose thumbnail size, photo background, emoji pack, and menu labels separately. Emoji packs let you choose the style of interface icons.

**Photo background → Off-white** changes the canvas behind transparent photos without editing the saved image. Choose a main photo or adjust its framing on the piece page; see [photos](USING-CLOSET-LINT.md#photos).

### Where settings are saved

Theme and other display preferences save in this browser. They do not sync between devices or travel in wardrobe backups. Opening the app at a different address may give you separate preferences.

Closet name, Linty visibility, category choices, and laundry tracking save with the wardrobe. Categories containing saved pieces stay available. **Show all categories…** lets you choose outside the starter list.

**Reset display preferences** restores the default appearance. Your wardrobe and size reference are kept.

## Style Sky

Sky uses your owned wardrobe for Sun, favorites and wishlist for Moon, and recorded wear for Rising. Sparse information produces a provisional reading. Choose a star or constellation to explore its contributing pieces.

**Share my sky** prepares a chart image without garment photos or your private installation address. Save the image, or use Share when your browser supports it. These actions do not publish your closet.

## Linty’s daily card

The optional daily card offers a fortune, wardrobe nudge, or color omen. Flip it to reveal the message, dismiss it for the day, or switch it off in settings. Wardrobe suggestions depend on the recorded pieces eligible for that card.
