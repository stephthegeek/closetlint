## Browse and collections

Browse remembers your sort order and direction in this browser. Random gets a fresh shuffle after reloading; searches and filters start clear.

Filter your closet by category, tags, favorites, and other recorded details. Open a collection to browse its pieces. **All** and **Closet** leave the collection filter. Selecting the site title or logo returns to My closet and clears search and temporary scopes such as Storage, Hidden, or a collection, while keeping Favorites and your sort order. Browser Back restores the previous view.

Use the **…** menus on piece, outfit, collection, and trip cards for more actions. **Duplicate collection** opens a new collection with the same description and pieces; review it and save to keep an independent copy.

## Piece details

**Wear this as a…** lets you choose whether a piece works as a main top, under another top, or over another top. Select every way you wear it. Use **Needs another clothing layer** to record required coverage, such as a base underneath a sheer top; outfit suggestions use both choices.

Open a piece to edit its name, photos, category, size, and other details. Categories and subcategories organize the kinds of pieces in your closet. Tags describe qualities such as color, fabric, style, occasion, and season, helping you filter your closet and find outfit combinations.

Add the details that are useful to you and leave unknown information blank. Layering choices describe how a piece can be worn with others; fabric weight and stretch describe how it feels and fits. **Metal & finish** adds metal tones and finishes to jewelry, watches, and bags, such as rose gold, copper, or antiqued metal.

Use the link button beside a saved product URL to open the original listing.

## Deleted pieces

**Delete** keeps a piece and its photos in **Deleted pieces**, linked at the bottom of your closet. Choose **Restore** to return it. For permanent removal, choose **Select for permanent deletion**, select pieces, and review the photo count before confirming. Pieces used in saved work or wear history are protected; shared photos remain while needed. Permanent deletion cannot be undone. Existing backups and photo request files are unchanged.

## Outfit Lab

In the **Outfit Lab**, choose **Occasion** under **Adjust the experiment** to focus suggestions. **Any** keeps the usual range. A focused suggestion includes a piece tagged for that occasion and compatible supporting pieces; if no complete outfit matches, change the occasion or other settings.

Use the refresh arrow on a main outfit piece to try another while keeping the remaining pieces and accessories. Your anchor stays fixed. If no compatible replacement matches your settings, the outfit stays in place. **Back** restores the previous combination.

**Doesn’t work** avoids the specific pair you choose. You can allow that pair again in **My taste → Pairs that don’t work**, or use **Back** to undo the action. Use **No**, **Like**, or **Love** to rate the whole outfit.

Lab suggestions use recorded colors, clothing details, and your preferences. Required underlayers favor smaller coverage pieces, such as a cami under a sheer top or a slip under a sheer dress. Suitable tights or stockings can meet a shoe’s sock requirement; footless leggings cannot. A required supporting piece stays in the outfit unless another piece provides that coverage.

Season choices affect clothing coverage and footwear as well as color. Winter favors warm clothing, requires long sleeves among the main layers, and avoids open footwear. Spring and fall cover short hems without adding a sweater to every outfit. In winter, **Accessorize** can suggest a few coordinating extras, such as a scarf, hat, gloves, or coat. Lab uses the seasons you select, not your location or local forecast.

## Outfit board

Select a piece, text, color strip, or collage window, then choose **Adjust** for movement, size or zoom, and rotation controls. You can also use arrow keys, plus/minus, and square brackets on the focused object. Hold **Shift** for larger steps on pieces and text; keys in a text field still edit its value.

For an outline on the board, choose **Adjust → Edit outline**, then add or move points and choose **Finish outline**. Cancel keeps the previous outline. Photo cropping has separate **Adjust crop** controls; **Apply crop** saves the photo while retaining the original.

Use **Undo** or **Ctrl/Command + Z** to step back through moves, resizing, rotation, text, styling, and removals. History is kept for the current editing session. Undo autosaves the working draft; choose **Save changes** to update a saved outfit.

Arrange pieces on the board, then choose **Save outfit**. **Save changes** updates an outfit you are editing; **Save a copy** creates a separate outfit.

Autosave keeps your working board, but does not update a saved outfit. **Drafts → Resume** returns to work kept when switching boards. Wait for successful autosave before closing; drafts are included in full wardrobe backups. If a save fails, keep the tab open and use Retry.

Opening a saved outfit shows its preview and leaves your current board in place. Choose **Edit outfit** to work on it. You can also log a wear or export its image from the preview.

Saved outfit thumbnails show the complete board design, including text, backgrounds, frames, piece styles, and color strips. The same composition appears in outfit lists, calendars, and trip previews.

## Wear logging and the calendar

Log a saved outfit with **Wore it**, or log individual pieces from their own pages. Scheduling an outfit is a plan, not a wear log.

Open a calendar day to see its outfits and other worn pieces, log an outfit, or manage that day’s records. **Save as outfit** creates an outfit from the individually logged pieces shown for that day. Removing a day from an outfit also removes the piece dates added by that outfit, while preserving independently recorded or shared dates. Individual piece dates can be managed separately. On a past day, choose **Copy to today** from a piece’s **…** menu to log it again without changing the original date.

Calendar thumbnails give a quick preview of each day. **+** shows how many additional pieces or outfits are logged; open the day to see everything.

Piece pages show the last 10 days of wear and a count for the last 30 days. Browse cards show an icon for hot pieces; **Hot** and **Very hot** filters help find them. 🔥 starts at three logged days in thirty days; ❤️‍🔥 starts at eight. Wears can be on any days; they do not need to be consecutive. Use **Today** to add or remove an individual wear. For a wear recorded through an outfit, open the day’s details to manage it.

On a future calendar day, create a dated outfit or schedule one you have saved. **Save & schedule** saves the new outfit and its date together. Returning to the calendar without saving does not schedule it.

### Schedule outfits

Open **Outfits → Schedule** to switch between **Agenda** and **Month**. Choose **Plan outfit** to assign a saved outfit to a date, with an optional event name. Trip outfits assigned to dates appear here too; open the trip wardrobe to change those plans. Planning an outfit does not mark it worn.

## Batch workspaces

**Add → Bulk import pieces** and **Menu → Utilities → Tidy photos** open separate pages. Leaving a page keeps its unfinished review. Check the draft notice before closing or reloading: it confirms recovery is stored or offers Retry.

These browser drafts belong to this browser and installation. They do not transfer between devices and are not included in wardrobe backups. If draft storage fails, finish the work or use the offered download/recovery options before closing. **Start over** or **Choose pieces again** discards unfinished review after confirmation, without removing already saved pieces.

Direct imports keep their saved progress on the installation. See [AI assistant imports](AI-ASSISTANT.md#ai-assistant-imports) for continuation and duplicate handling.

## Bookmarks, tabs, and devices

Bookmark or copy the address shown by the app to return to a piece, outfit, collection, trip, or calendar month. The address belongs to that installation; sharing it does not share or copy the wardrobe.

Back and Forward follow your navigation. Browse filters and scroll position can return with browser history; a newly opened bookmark does not necessarily reproduce those temporary filters.

Links support the browser’s Open in new tab action. A new tab opens saved data, without copying unsaved edits. Save before switching devices. If an older tab refuses a save, preserve its unfinished work before refreshing.

On home Wi-Fi, use the address printed by the wardrobe computer. On a phone, `localhost` refers to the phone itself. See [installation](GETTING-STARTED.md#start-the-app) for phone access.


## Photos

An ordinary photo is enough to add a piece. Use your camera, a screenshot, or an available product photo. You can keep several views, including model, isolated, detail, and care label photos.

### Main photos and framing

Open a piece and expand **Photo options** to add or replace photos, crop them, adjust swatches, or choose the main photo. Viewing a gallery thumbnail does not change the main photo.

The main photo appears in your closet and on boards. **Thumbnail framing** lets you show the whole photo or fill its frame with a crop. Tap the large photo to enlarge it.

Each piece can have up to 12 photos, including cutouts and care labels. Saved images are limited to 1600 pixels on their longest edge. Keep camera originals separately if you need full resolution. Full wardrobe backups include all saved photo versions.

### Optional background removal

Background removal uses a model suited to fine details for Jewelry and Eyewear. Review each cutout before saving; your original stays available. See [optional photo tools](GETTING-STARTED.md#optional-photo-tools) for setup.

Background removal creates a transparent cutout and keeps the original. It does not redraw a garment or remove a person wearing it. Choose **Remove background**, review the result, then keep the cutout or discard it. Lace, fine jewelry, and sheer fabric can be difficult; keeping the original is fine.

You can also start background removal from a selected board piece. Accepting a cutout makes it the piece’s main photo throughout Closet Lint; switch back from the piece page if needed.

For several pieces, open **Menu → Utilities → Tidy photos**. Choose **Most recent** or **Oldest** to set the order. Review the current photo and cutout for each piece, choose what to keep, and save. Pieces left on Current photo are not suggested again in Tidy photos; you can still retry from their piece pages. Pieces whose main photo is already a cutout are skipped.

Wait for the draft notice before closing an unfinished review. Draft recovery belongs to this browser and is separate from wardrobe backups. See [batch workspaces](USING-CLOSET-LINT.md#batch-workspaces).

### Care label references

Open **Care → Add care label photo**, or mark an existing view **Care label** in Photo options. Washing, Drying, and Care note are optional. Photo uploads save immediately; other form edits save with **Save changes**.

Care labels can be enlarged but are excluded from main garment photos, boards, swatches, Tidy photos, and background removal. They count toward the 12-photo limit and are included in backups. Removing a photo offers Undo.

For optional tool installation, see [Getting started](GETTING-STARTED.md#start-the-app). For individual product photo requests, see [AI assistant photo preparation](AI-ASSISTANT.md#prepare-individual-photos-for-existing-pieces).

## Tidy titles and tags

Open **Menu → Utilities → Tidy titles & tags**, or select pieces in Browse and choose **Titles & tags**. Ask your AI assistant for shorter titles, missing tags, or both. Use direct access if the assistant can reach your installation, or download a request ZIP and return its results file.

Review the suggestions in one list. Edit titles or uncheck changes you do not want, then choose **Save selected changes**. Checked tag suggestions can replace existing tags. Original listing titles remain searchable; photos, ownership, and wear history are kept. **Prepare next batch** continues with more pieces. An unfinished review stays in the same browser; check the draft notice before closing.

## Split a multipack

Open the piece and choose **Split**. An explicit listing count such as “5pcs” fills in the review; otherwise enter the count. Keep normal pairs together, and check each piece’s name detail and color.

Choose whether to prepare individual photos with your AI assistant, crop them yourself, or keep the shared photo. Confirm the split to replace the pack with its separate pieces and continue to the chosen photo step. Cropping keeps the original photo available; AI assistant photos are reviewed before saving.

## Trips and packing

Open **Menu → Trips** to gather pieces and outfits and build a packing list. A trip can also cover a local occasion where you want a planned wardrobe. For everyday plans or a single event, use **Outfits → Schedule → Plan outfit**; the event name is optional.

Outfits pinned to a particular trip day appear in Schedule. Use **Open trip wardrobe** there to change their assignments in the trip planner. Both views use the same saved plan. Outfits pinned for any day stay in the trip wardrobe until you assign them a date.

Choose **Duplicate trip** from a trip’s **…** menu to reuse its settings, dates, and outfit plan. The copy opens for review with packing checkmarks cleared. Change its name or dates, then save; the original trip stays separate.

### Plan a trip

1. Enter a name, destination, and dates. A name is enough to save unfinished setup and return later.
2. Choose the day types, extra evening outfits, bag, and laundry availability. Adjust individual days when plans differ.
3. Choose which pieces to consider. You can include saved outfits, stored pieces, and wishlist pieces, and add pieces or outfits you are bringing for sure.
4. Build the suggestions. Review them, swap pieces, and pin the outfits you intend to bring.

Only pinned outfits go into a new trip’s packing list. Explicitly added pieces and selected basics are included too. Suggestions are not marked packed automatically. Save failures keep your choices available for Retry.

### Weather and suitability

Typical weather uses recent seasonal averages, not a forecast. Check the destination and temperatures, or enter them yourself. Consider your activities and the range between daytime and evening conditions.

Matching uses the details in your closet. A shoe category does not establish walking comfort or waterproofing, and a style tag does not guarantee a dress code is met. Review suggested clothing and equipment for your actual plans.

### Basics and activities

The planner suggests underwear, bras, socks, and sleepwear based on the pieces recorded in your closet. Review these basics and adjust them to your routine.

Quantities are starting points. Use **Choose** to change the pieces or quantity; zero omits that group. Missing matches refer to the catalog and selected filters, rather than everything you own.

Add swimming or exercise to include reminders for those activities. Check that you have the pieces you need for each activity, including anything you have not added to your closet.

Laundry availability reduces suggested quantities. Adjust them to when you can wash and dry your clothes. Extra evening outfits use dressy suggestions.

### Pack and export

Check items off as you pack. **Wear on the way** separates suggested travel clothing from bag contents; change the bulky item travel option if it does not suit you. Stored and wishlist pieces have separate pull/buy reminders.

The bag bar estimates space for clothing. Allow room for toiletries and other belongings, and check luggage dimensions and weight separately.

Use **Export packing list** to print, save a PDF through your browser, or copy/download Markdown text. Print/PDF can include small photos; outfit combinations are optional in both formats. The export reflects the current packing selection.

Changed trip choices may need **Rebuild suggestions** before the list reflects them. Rebuilding keeps pinned outfits; check quantities and other explicit choices afterward.

## Wishlist

Your choices set the ranking order. Arrange pieces in **Ranking**, or compare two at a time in **Head-to-head**, with their recorded prices. Use **Planned**, **Want**, and **Someday** to organize your intentions.

Evidence chips summarize matches and cautions from recorded piece details, your closet, Lab ratings, wear history, and Comfort zone answers. Green chips group things you like or love; orange chips flag dislikes, with a darker orange for your strongest dislikes. A purple heart highlights multiple matching Obsessed or top three preferences. General clothing color dislikes do not apply to jewelry; jewelry preferences come from its own quiz.

**Fills a gap** highlights things you like but own little of, including traits that are underrepresented in a larger closet. **Expands your style** highlights a confidently matched style connected to your Style Sky. These give a small ranking boost; stored pieces count when checking gaps.

Open **Pair this with…** for suggested partners. Missing details limit the evidence, and suggestions still need your judgment.

## My almanac

Before your first wear, **My almanac → Start your week in color** opens today’s wear logging. The menu preview shows the last seven days, ending today. After that, open **My almanac** to see your month of logged wears in color. Days before your first logged wear are not counted as missed. Select a day to review it or fill in a missed wear. Future dates open the same day details, where you can create an outfit or plan one you have saved. You can also see your next trip and revisit outfits you loved in the Lab. **My next quiz** lets you start or resume a Comfort zone quiz; **All quizzes** opens the full list.

Calendar dots and the menu week use the photo swatches from the pieces you logged, including past wear logs. Saved swatches take priority; otherwise swatches are calculated from the main photo. Pieces without usable swatches contribute their tagged colors instead. The month image uses the same colors.

Choose your city in **Settings** for local daylight, weather, and lunar events in **Today’s Sky**, alongside a daily wardrobe suggestion. Color names in palette readings link to matching pieces in your closet. You can leave the city blank and use the wear calendar on its own.

**Upcoming trip** and **Next scheduled outfit** show your next trip and a scheduled outfit event in separate rows. Open either summary for its details. Use **All trips** to open your trip wardrobes or **Open schedule** for scheduled outfits and events.

**My taste** alternates each day between your most recently loved Lab outfit and a rotating card from your taste analysis: a positive pattern, a piece you keep loving, or a pattern in passed outfits. The daily card stays the same when you refresh. Open an insight to see the rated outfits behind it. Insights appear after at least eight valid ratings; until then, your latest loved outfit stays on display.

The **Sizes & fit** tab keeps your clothing, shoe, bra, and ring sizes with your measurement history; **Settings** contains your closet and display preferences.

## Comfort zone

Open **Profile → Comfort zone** for eight fun, short quizzes about fabrics, fit, colors, shoes, jewelry, and more. Explore what feels like you; your answers help shape wishlist rankings and Outfit Lab ideas.

Try whichever topics interest you, skip anything, and return to change your answers anytime. At the end of each quiz, you can optionally highlight up to three favorites. There is no need to finish every quiz to get useful suggestions.

## Themes and settings

Open **Menu → Settings**, or the **Settings** tab in Profile. **Outfit naming style** changes the voice of suggested names in the Lab and on the board; names you have already saved stay as they are.

Under **Closet tabs**, choose up to three extra category tabs. **All**, **Clothing**, **Accessories**, and **Off-duty** always stay available. **Jewelry** is the default extra tab. Category tabs can overlap with the standard views; they do not move or change your pieces. Tab preferences save in this browser.

- **Soft Hardware:** pixel accents and monospace controls.
- **Field Notes:** warm paper colors and serif headings.
- **Night Bloom:** plum colors and arched photo frames.

Each theme offers light, dark, and system appearance. Choose thumbnail size, photo background, emoji pack, and menu labels separately. Emoji packs let you choose the style of interface icons.

**Photo background → Off-white** changes the canvas behind transparent photos without editing the saved image. Choose a main photo or adjust its framing on the piece page; see [photos](USING-CLOSET-LINT.md#photos).

### Where settings are saved

Theme and other display preferences save in this browser. They do not sync between devices or travel in wardrobe backups. Opening the app at a different address may give you separate preferences.

Closet name, Linty visibility, category choices, and laundry tracking save with the wardrobe. Categories containing saved pieces stay available. **Show all categories…** lets you choose outside the starter list.

**Reset display preferences** restores the default appearance. Your wardrobe and size reference are kept.

## Style Sky

Sky uses your owned wardrobe for Sun, favorites and wishlist for Moon, and recorded wear for Rising. Sparse information produces a provisional reading. Choose a star or constellation to explore its contributing pieces.

**Share my sky** prepares a chart image without garment photos or your private installation address. Save the image, or use Share when your browser supports it. These actions do not publish your closet.

## Linty’s daily card

The optional daily card offers a fortune, wardrobe nudge, or color omen. Flip it to reveal the message, dismiss it for the day, or switch it off in settings. Wardrobe suggestions depend on the recorded pieces eligible for that card.

### Outfit naming style

In **Profile → Settings → Preferences**, choose **Outfit naming style**. Surprise me mixes styles; Descriptive uses garment names and colors. Goblin mode, Twee Much, Nerdy, Editorial, and Poetic offer different voices. The preference saves with your closet and applies to new suggestions in the Lab and board. Use the existing dice buttons for another suggestion. Saved names stay as you chose them.
