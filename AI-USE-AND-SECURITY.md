# AI use and security

Closet Lint is an AI-assisted project. stephthegeek directs the product, design, copy, and visual review. AI assistants have written substantial portions of the code, tests, and documentation.

Automated checks and test wardrobes help verify imports, saving, updates, and recovery. Review records describe what was checked and the remaining limits; they do not establish that the software has no defects. There has not been an independent human security audit. See [testing](CONTRIBUTING.md#testing) and [security](SECURITY.md).

AI is optional when using Closet Lint. You choose whether to share sources or wardrobe information with your own assistant. The app does not connect to your inbox or include an AI provider account or API key. Background removal uses a separately downloaded model and keeps the saved original.

The sample photos, Comfort zone quiz images, and Linty artwork were created with generative image tools. Quiz images illustrate choices; they do not depict products offered for sale. Asset information and licenses are recorded in [NOTICE](NOTICE) and the asset folders. Your wardrobe data and photos remain yours.

Report security problems through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/stephthegeek/closetlint/security/advisories/new), rather than a public issue.
